## Start from the body, not a menu

Points turns a guided practice into a point by point session. Instead of beginning with a generic category list, you choose a point on an interactive body map and use that choice to focus the session.

Each point creates a small ritual: settle in, choose what you are carrying that day, and let the interaction guide your attention from there.

## Why I made it

I wanted to explore whether the ritual and focus of acupuncture could become a calming at home experience without needles.
