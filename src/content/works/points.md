## Start from the body, not a menu

Points turns a guided practice into a point-by-point session. Instead of choosing a mood or category from a list, you select a point on an interactive body map — head, chest, hands, knees — and that choice guides the focus of the session.

Each point holds its own session: settle in, choose what you're carrying that day, and let the point lead from there.
