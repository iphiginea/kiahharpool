## Draw it. Clue it. See if they get it.

Duel Draw is a two player browser drawing game. One player creates a room and sends the code to a friend; the other joins and the pair takes turns sketching and guessing.

The experience is deliberately small and direct: one other person, one shared game, and no giant public lobby between you.

## Why I made it

I wanted a drawing game that felt like playing directly with one person instead of entering a noisy public room.
