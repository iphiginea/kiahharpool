## Sketch, guess, no rush

Duel Draw is a real-time two-player drawing game. One player creates a room and sends the code to a friend; the other joins with that code. From there it's sketch and guess, pulling from a deck of 100 prompts with no repeats until the deck cycles.

There's no timer pressure — draw whenever you're ready, guess whenever you're ready.
