## Your life, in objects

Acquired turns the things you keep into a personal museum collection. Instead of saving another photograph and losing the story around it, you accession an object with a permanent catalog number and record what it is, where it came from, and why it matters.

Each collection can hold photographs alongside museum-style fields for date, maker, classification, materials, place, acquisition history, provenance, and personal significance. Objects can be kept private or made public individually.

## Built like a collection database

Accounts and collection data are backed by Supabase, with authentication, structured object records, and image storage. Each new object receives a collection-specific accession number, and uploaded photographs can be cropped for the collection grid or processed to remove their background.

The collection view is searchable and designed to grow over time, while individual object pages preserve the fuller record behind each thumbnail.

## A museum language for personal history

The interface borrows from collection catalogs rather than social feeds: cream paper tones, archival red, brass accession numbers, editorial serif type, fine rules, and intentionally quiet object cards.

The goal is not to quantify ownership. It is to make the history attached to ordinary things feel worth documenting — one object at a time.
