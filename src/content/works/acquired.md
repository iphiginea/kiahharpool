## Your life, in objects

Acquired is a personal museum for the things that are easy to keep and hard to explain. Instead of letting an object become another unlabeled photograph, you accession it into a collection with a permanent catalog number and a record of what it is, where it came from, and why it matters.

The idea came from museum collection systems: objects become more meaningful when the story around them is preserved too. Acquired borrows that structure and applies it to ordinary personal history.

## Accession first, organize second

Every object gets a collection-specific accession number and a structured record for title, date, maker, classification, materials, place, acquisition history, provenance, and personal significance.

Photographs can be uploaded, cropped for the collection grid, and processed to remove their background. Objects can stay private or be made public individually, so the collection can hold both personal records and things worth sharing.

## A collection that can keep growing

Supabase handles accounts, authentication, object records, and image storage. The collection view is searchable and designed to scale as more objects are added, while each object page keeps the complete record behind the thumbnail.

The interface deliberately feels closer to a museum catalog than a social feed: cream paper tones, archival red, brass accession numbers, serif display type, and thin collection-label rules.

The point is not to document everything you own. It is to preserve the things whose stories would otherwise disappear.
